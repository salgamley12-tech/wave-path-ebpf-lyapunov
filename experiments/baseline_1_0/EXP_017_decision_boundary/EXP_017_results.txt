EXP-017 — Decision Boundary Comparison

Objective: Compare legacy and current risk assessment using identical inputs.

Cases: 5
NEGATIVE: old=low new=LOW match=True
ZERO: old=low new=LOW match=True
SMALL_POS: old=high new=MEDIUM match=False
LARGE_POS: old=high new=HIGH match=True
ANOMALY: old=high new=LOW match=False

Matches: 3
Mismatches: 2
Status: DIFFERENCE_DETECTED

Key observations:
1. The legacy classifier treats any positive dVdt as HIGH risk.
2. The current Lyapunov classifier distinguishes MEDIUM and HIGH according to dVdt/V.
3. The legacy classifier incorporates anomaly_score.
4. The current Lyapunov classifier does not currently incorporate anomaly_score.
5. Therefore the two classifiers are not semantically equivalent.

Boundary:
EXP-017 validates the existence of a decision-boundary difference.
It does not establish which classifier is universally preferable.
No production classifier was modified by this experiment.

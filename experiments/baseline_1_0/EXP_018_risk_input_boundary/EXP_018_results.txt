EXP-018 — Risk Input Boundary Comparison

Objective:
Determine whether anomaly_score independently affects the legacy
and current risk assessment paths under identical V and dVdt inputs.

Cases:
LOW_ANOMALY: legacy=low current=LOW anomaly_score=0.1
HIGH_ANOMALY: legacy=high current=LOW anomaly_score=0.9
POSITIVE_DVDT_LOW_ANOMALY: legacy=high current=MEDIUM anomaly_score=0.1
POSITIVE_DVDT_HIGH_ANOMALY: legacy=high current=MEDIUM anomaly_score=0.9

Observation:
The legacy classifier incorporates anomaly_score.
The current Lyapunov risk assessment does not accept anomaly_score
as an independent input.

Changing anomaly_score from 0.1 to 0.9 while keeping V and dVdt
constant changed the legacy classification but did not change the
current Lyapunov classification.

Status:
COMPLETED

Methodological boundary:
EXP-018 validates an implementation-level input boundary.
It does not establish which risk model is universally preferable.
No production classifier was modified.
